## Scope

One-page Persuade surface (index.html) for New Dents Mauá. Visitor decides and acts: tap WhatsApp to book an evaluation. Audience, proof and constraints live in PRODUCT.md; copy and section list were approved by the user at the taste-skill checkpoint (CriadorDeSites.md, PRIMEIRO-SITE mode).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the clinic's own logo drawing itself, then talking like the reception does. The client's animated tooth is the one authored moment; everything else stays calm, white-grounded and human. Refuses the dental-site default (stock smile photos, teal-on-white, three icon cards).

OWN-WORLD: Off-white ground (#fcfafd) tinted toward violet, one deep violet (#5f2a6e, sampled from the logo) as the only accent, lilac tints (#f5eff9, #eaddf2) for quiet fields, ink #2a1235. Bricolage Grotesque display over Figtree body. Pill buttons, 28px tiles, 24px media. Committed violet fields for the rating band and the closing CTA. No photography besides the real facade.

STORY: A parent or adult from Mauá, arriving from Instagram on a phone, sees the brand's own slogan and the tooth drawing itself, learns in one band that 380 Google reviews average 4.9, skims four real treatment areas, reads five real patient quotes that keep mentioning the reception, finds the address and hours, and taps "Agendar avaliação" at any point.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the client slogan as a 5rem two-line-plus display headline with "sorrisos" in violet, one sentence of support, one pill CTA and a quiet text link. Right, the client's logo animation, scaled 1.3 and feathered so its white blends into the page. The Google rating band begins at the fold.

FORM: Pinned by brief, not rolled: CriadorDeSites.md template CLEAN with the brand violet, approved at checkpoint. Roll 746453fe assigned a catalog challenger that cannot carry the product's truth, and a user- or brief-pinned decision beats the roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
